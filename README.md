# Allen E. Sweigert, Sr. — WWII & Family History

An illustrated oral history of Master Sergeant Allen Earl Sweigert, Sr.
(9 September 1918 – 29 March 2012), 90th Bombardment Squadron, 3rd Bombardment
Group, "The Grim Reapers", Fifth Air Force.

It is built on two home-video tapes his grandson recorded in the summer of 2006,
set against more than three hundred photographs and documents from Allen's own
collection, his wartime letters, his service record book, and the county
newspapers that followed him from a Boy Scout excursion in 1931 to a stolen flag
in 2011.

Every claim in it says how it is known. Where the record contradicts him, both
are printed. Where nothing settles a question, it says so.
