Allen E. Sweigert, Sr. · WWII & Family History

Pennsylvania National Guard

1936 – 1940, Battery F, 213th Coast Artillery

Before the Air Force

Allen's first military service, before the Army Air Force: Pennsylvania National Guard weekend drills and summer encampments, 1936 to 1940.

“The First Defenders”

The nickname predates the 213th itself. When Lincoln called for troops on April 15, 1861, five Pennsylvania militia companies reached Washington, D.C. within two days — among the first armed Union units to arrive, earning the state's volunteers the name “First Defenders.” Two of those companies were Pottsville's own: the Washington Artillerists and the National Light Infantry. In 1922 those two units were consolidated into a new Pennsylvania National Guard regiment, the 213th Coast Artillery (Anti-Aircraft), carrying the First Defenders name and lineage forward — the regiment, and Battery F, that Allen joined in 1936. After Allen transferred to the Army Air Force in April 1941, the 213th itself deployed overseas as part of Operation Torch, fighting in North Africa and Italy, before being broken up in an April 1944 reorganization of anti-aircraft units.

Sources: Pennsylvania First Defenders; Schuylkill County Historical Society; 213th Coast Artillery Regiment archives.
